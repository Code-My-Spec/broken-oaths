# Day 3: you can't lose Broken Oaths, you can only be conquered

Broken Oaths is a persistent 4X on a real hex globe. Take someone's last city and they don't lose the game. They become your vassal.

Today the other half went live. As a vassal you pay tribute and your oath strain climbs, and when you have had enough you gather the other vassals in secret and rise together. Hold your freed cities for ten turns and the oath breaks for good.

The clip is one run: I conquer a rival and make them mine, then turn around and break free from the tyrant who owns me.

Free to play, in your browser, at broken-oaths.com.

Next up is the age ladder, from stone-age raids all the way to intergalactic colonies. Roadmap at broken-oaths.com/roadmap.
