BROKEN OATHS — demo cut (~75s)

Before you record — the staged world opens on the "Terms of Oath" screen (you're
the tyrant's vassal; your last free city has fallen). Pick any Hidden Agenda to
dismiss it onto the board — the choice is secret and changes nothing downstream.
Do this OFF-CAMERA: the "you're a vassal too" turn is the Beat 2 reveal, so you
open on the conquest, not on being conquered.

Cold open (0–5s) — Board open, slowly rotate the globe. Caption:

▎ One living world. It keeps turning while you're offline.

Title (5–8s) — Caption card:

▎ BROKEN OATHS — a real-time 4X on a hex globe

Beat 1 · Conquer → Vassalize (8–25s) — Select warrior #496, attack City 1 (#19) (one hit → breaks, 0 counter). /step once, move onto the tile to occupy. Rival appears in your Vassals panel. Captions, timed to the actions:

▎ Take a rival's last city… → …and they don't die. → They swear fealty. Now they're your vassal.

Beat 2 · The strained oath (25–40s) — Open Vassals (your fresh vassal, low strain), then your oath panel: my-oath-strain 90/100, tribute maxed, tyrant honor floored. Captions:

▎ But you're a vassal too — under a tyrant. → Honor: 0. Tribute: maxed. Oath strain: 90/100.

Beat 3 · Break the oath (40–55s) — Open the independence preview (capital will_rise?: true, army preview 10 units). Confirm → city rises, rebel army spawns, at-war-with, rebellion-panel. Captions:

▎ So you break it. → Declare independence. Your city rises. An army spawns. War.

Beat 4 · Win (55–70s) — /step ten times, watch rebellion-panel count the hold → independence_won. Captions:

▎ Hold ten turns… → Independence won. The oath is severed for good.

End card (70–75s):

▎ You walked in as someone's property. You walked out a free power — with a vassal of your own.
