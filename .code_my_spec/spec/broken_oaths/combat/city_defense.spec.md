# BrokenOaths.Combat.CityDefense

City combat math and garrison rules: city HP (max 100) and defensive strength (20 + 5 × size + garrison defense), the 3-military-unit garrison stacking exception (civilians shelter freely without counting), +50% garrison defensive bonus and fighting from the walls, garrison counter-attacks, 5 HP/turn regeneration when unthreatened, pillage-not-capture at 0 HP (−1 population, production halted 3 turns, HP resets to 50), and approach/under-attack alerts.
