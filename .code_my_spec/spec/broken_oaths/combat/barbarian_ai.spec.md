# BrokenOaths.Combat.BarbarianAI

Per-boundary barbarian decisions: aggro targeting (nearest player unit or city within 5 hexes, undefended cities preferred), 1-hex movement toward the target, roaming near the home camp when nothing is in range, attack-on-sight against adjacent player units, never attacking other barbarians, and pillaging completed improvements on entry (yields stop until a worker repairs in 1 turn). Kills by players pay a 10-gold bounty (paid at resolution).
