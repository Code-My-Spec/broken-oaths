# BrokenOaths.Combat.Resolver

Pure combat core: effective strength (linear wounded penalty from 100% at full HP to 50% near death), the Civ VI damage curve (30 base at equal strength, ~4%/point, ±25% roll), simultaneous attacker/defender exchange from pre-combat state, lord +2 adjacency aura (attack and defense), flat-strength damage against camps, adjacency and target-legality validation (barbarians only in the Stone Age), and combat-result reporting.
