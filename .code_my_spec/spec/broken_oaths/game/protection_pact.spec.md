# BrokenOaths.Game.ProtectionPact

Protection Pact engine (story 914). Raises a protection call when a vassal's city/units come under attack by a third party, tracks the lord's response window, and scores the outcome: honoring builds the lord's Honor and eases Oath Strain; letting the call expire is a broken oath that takes a public Honor hit and spikes strain. Reputational/relational enforcement only (no hard lock). Depends on Vassalage, Honor (Tribute/Player), OathStrain, and the Stewardship defend-when-attacked rule. Behind :feudal_enabled.
