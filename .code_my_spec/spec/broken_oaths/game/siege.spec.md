# BrokenOaths.Game.Siege

PvP city siege and capture. Adjacency and legality for a military unit attacking a hostile PLAYER city (opens PvP, previously barbarians-only); multi-turn HP grind composing Combat and CityDefense with city counter-attacks and owner relief; the capture flow — zeroing HP breaks the city, then moving a unit onto the city tile occupies it (marks the City occupied by the captor); fallen-garrison execute-or-release choice with a small Honor delta; fires the last-free-city check that drives Vassalization. Story 906.
