# BrokenOaths.Game.Research

The Ancient-era tech tree: eleven Civ-6-accurate techs with prerequisite edges (Pottery 50, Animal Husbandry 50, Mining 75, Sailing 90, Astrology 90 with no prerequisite; Writing 90 and Irrigation 90 after Pottery; Archery 90 after Animal Husbandry; Masonry 100, The Wheel 100, and Bronze Working 100 after Mining), science generated by city population (2/pop/turn), one tech researched at a time with banked-per-tech progress, prerequisite gating on selection (`set_research/2` refuses a tech whose prerequisites aren't all completed), and applying each tech's unlock on completion (including advancing to the Bronze Age on Bronze Working, now reachable only after Mining).
