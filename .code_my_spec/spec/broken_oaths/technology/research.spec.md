# BrokenOaths.Technology.Research

The Ancient-era tech tree: eleven Civ-6-accurate techs with prerequisite edges (Pottery 80, Animal Husbandry 80, Mining 110, Sailing 150, Astrology 150 with no prerequisite; Writing 150 and Irrigation 150 after Pottery; Archery 150 after Animal Husbandry; Masonry 240, The Wheel 240, and Bronze Working 240 after Mining — costs rebalanced per QA issue d95ea179 "Research rebalance", proportional to Civ 6's own ancient-era cost curve), science generated by city population (2/pop/turn), one tech researched at a time with banked-per-tech progress, prerequisite gating on selection (`set_research/2` refuses a tech whose prerequisites aren't all completed), and applying each tech's unlock on completion (including advancing to the Bronze Age on Bronze Working, now reachable only after Mining).
