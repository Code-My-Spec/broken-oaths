# July 18, 2026: Four Releases and the Feudal Turn

Thirty-three commits, nineteen hours of wall clock, and four tagged releases. The day that took Broken Oaths from a prototype to a versioned product also spent its evening building the mechanic the whole game is named after.

## Morning: shipping the Stone Age

It started at 4:49 a.m. with the biggest commit in the project so far. Batch 5 (`ae2d940`) landed 147 files and roughly 8,000 insertions: a research tree, the Bronze Age transition, progress and age panels, tile resources with per-world density, and five migrations. Story-level QA had already blessed it.

Cross-story QA had other ideas. Four end-to-end journeys, run as a real player rather than as a test, immediately found three bugs the per-story passes had missed. The screenshot names are the receipt: `j2_05_BUG_city_unreachable.png` and `j3_09_BUG_over_cap_worked_tiles.png` sit in `e0f7c40`. Fixed, rerun 4/4 clean (`b409d27`), then the four journeys were frozen into permanent regression tests (`5587e30`). By 9:17 a.m., v0.2.0, the Stone Age MVP, was in production (`3576d60`).

## Midmorning: designing before building

The next three quarters of an hour produced no code at all. Five commits (`7b27418` through `922bc83`) grew `feudal_vassalage_design.md` in layers: prior art and a novel design pass first, then round-4 mechanics, then round-5 decisions recorded "post Three Amigos," then a gold bank and stewardship engagement layer. The last one is a six-line correction admitting stewardship should cover alliances, not just vassalage. Cheap to change on paper.

Then `9bcc11e`: 5,624 insertions across 69 files, of which 46 were executable BDD specs and only two were implementation. Sieges, vassalization, tribute, banking, and stewardship were fully specified before a line of siege logic existed.

## Afternoon: the release tax

Shipping to production invited players, and players found things. Four user-reported gameplay bugs (`f578c18`) forced v0.2.1 out at 12:49. Then the real playtest triage: backend fixes (`bb7a0c4`), frontend fixes (`62509c2`), and mobile rendering fixes (`883ea9f`), thirteen issues across three commits in about seventy minutes. v0.2.2 (`09bea33`) shipped the half-built feudal system to prod behind a dormant feature flag, with 276 lines of test proving the flag actually keeps it dormant.

Two commits quietly rewrote that morning's work. `579fd75` expanded four Stone Age techs into an eleven-tech Ancient tree with prerequisites and deleted the spec written twelve hours earlier (`..._presents_the_four_stone_age_techs_with_costs_spex.exs`, gone). `e0ceda9` retuned resource density with a spec literally titled "the world no longer feels like resources everywhere." Copper as a gating strategic resource followed (`9165723`), and v0.2.3 went out at 18:37.

## Night: feudalism, and one expensive shortcut

From 19:53 to 23:57, five commits turned the morning's specs into a playable system: Bank and Stewardship with panels (`252d7f5`), capture and garrison and levy UI plus a garrison ownership bug found in code six hours old (`5c7d565`), complete-capture and levy-refusal Honor (`8e65262`), and the stewardship click-through UI (`cfa8a07`).

The most instructive commit is `1dea523`. Tribute and banking had been built against placeholder income, so wiring them to real city gold yields meant touching 39 files and rewriting nineteen spex files authored that same morning. The specs held the shape; the stub underneath them did not. Note the `re2_` screenshot prefixes in the final commit: QA rounds 906 through 912 were on their second attempt by midnight.
