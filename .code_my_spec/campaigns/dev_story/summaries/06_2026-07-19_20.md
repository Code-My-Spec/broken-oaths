# July 19-20, 2026: Rebellion Ships, Then the Walls Come Down

Fifty-nine commits in forty-six hours, the densest stretch of the sprint. It opened with a patch and closed with an economy, and in between John tore the entire codebase apart and put it back in a different shape.

## Shipping first, at 1am

The period started mid-firefight. Copper had gone unreachable in production and broken the Bronze Spearman, so `7467b58` rewrote resource placement (345 lines of churn in `worlds/resources.ex`, a new `worlds/regions.ex`) and shipped as v0.2.4 (`c86e042`). By 9am, Feudal Vassalage was flipped on in prod as v0.3.0 (`fe848c4`) on the back of a passing re-QA of story 911 (`7286b99`), and v0.3.1 followed within the hour with roads that coexist with improvements plus the first Archer (`5375e9f`).

## Rebellion, in one afternoon

Then came the largest single commit of the project. `6f7dc27` landed the rebellion foundation: 59 files, 9,284 insertions, three migrations, and roughly forty executable spex files covering Oath Strain, Protection Pacts, and the Pact of Broken Oaths. Over the next three hours the mechanics went live one story at a time: Declare Independence and the rebellion lifecycle (`17031a5`), a tyrant-lord fixture and Honor test seam to green two stubborn 919 specs (`b466ceb`), Lord's Death and heir reconciliation (`c8b7ea2`), and coordinated multi-vassal revolt (`23ccf0c`).

All of it went into `world_server.ex`, and that was the problem.

## Eight slices and six reorgs

At 18:37, `23ccf0c`'s companion knowledge doc on GenServer decomposition became a plan. `33b2497` pulled 1,162 lines of feudal and rebellion logic out of WorldServer under the banner "pragdave logic-home," and seven more slices followed through the night: combat and vassalization, cities and production, unit orders and camp assault, social and vision, stewardship and levy and bank, `turn.ex` split into domain-owned tick phases (`c6fd8b5` cut it from 1,199 lines), and finally the 1,161-line `game.ex` facade split into per-domain sub-facades (`6f7f0af`). One detour was needed first: spex tests were leaking WorldServer state between runs, fixed in `e56f903` alongside a pinned `.tool-versions`.

That was structure inside `Game.*`. From 23:37 to 00:58, six more commits (`2124013` through `2343ee7`) promoted ten bounded contexts out of it entirely: Vision, Technology, Players, Cities, Units, Combat, Diplomacy, Feudal, Simulation. Pure moves, roughly 200 lines touched per commit, zero net change. At 4am the LiveViews got the same treatment, `play.ex` shedding 2,028 lines into extracted components and a view-model (`2ca18a3`), then the world builder (`6b2b2b8`). The specs were reconciled to match the next morning (`568a050`, 37 leaf specs relocated).

## The fixes that took three tries

Board panning oscillated. `95f83ed` coalesced redraws and dropped to low detail while panning, `26a5d60` walked that back for capable machines, and `509064d` finally scoped the degradation to touch pans only, never mouse. The Archer needed three passes too: shipped in `5375e9f`, given a real ranged action and a `Units.Actions` context in `6aa6963`, then made discoverable in the UI in `377861f`. Copper got a second fix (`495d3ca`) making access mine-based and player-wide. And `a974c18` split movement recharge onto an N-turn cadence, only for `8c23d38` to decouple healing from it thirteen minutes later.

Day two still closed strong: Fortify with Civ-6 ramps (`b8f480e`, `35e63cb`), the Galley as first naval unit (`4460190`, sprite fixed in `fd9f587`), a gold maintenance economy for units and buildings (`eeb0570`), a public roadmap page (`59493cd`), and boot-time migrations to close the deploy window (`5c2aa82`).
