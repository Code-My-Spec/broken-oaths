## Release notes

A full civilization arc on the hex globe, from a lone settler to the Bronze Age.

- **Start & explore** — spawn into an unexplored region with your Lord and Settler; automatic 60s turns (configurable per world); queue moves between turns; fog of war per player; regions guarantee room to expand.
- **Build** — found cities, set production (settlers, workers, warriors, monuments), grow cities by working tiles, improve terrain (farms, mines), and expand with new settlers.
- **Fight** — unit combat, barbarian camps that spawn and hunt, camp assault, city defense + garrison, and your Lord as a battlefield anchor.
- **Meet neighbors** — discovery notifications, real-time player-to-player chat, and cooperative barbarian fighting.
- **Advance** — a Stone Age tech tree (Animal Husbandry, Pottery, Mining, Bronze Working), the Bronze Age with the Bronze Spearman and a raised city size cap, a progress panel with milestones, and tile resources (Cattle/Sheep/Wheat/Stone) with Animal-Husbandry pastures.
