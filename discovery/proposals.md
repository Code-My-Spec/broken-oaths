## Proposal rules of thumb
- **First 2 lines = their problem, specific.** No "Dear Sir, I am expert in many CRM."
- **One scoping question** — shows you think, starts a conversation, gets a reply.
- **Offer the paid pilot** — de-risks hiring a newer profile; fits agency repeat-buyers.
- **Don't overpromise the AI** — "AI-assisted, fully QA'd by me" not "AI does it all."
- Send within an hour of the post when you can; early proposals get read.
