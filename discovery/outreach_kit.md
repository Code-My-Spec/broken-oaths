# Outreach Kit — talking to GHL migration buyers

## The constraint (read first)

Upwork **hides client identity** until you engage. You CANNOT cold-email a client from
a job post. Three real paths to reach these buyers:

1. **Apply to live jobs as a service** (costs Connects ~$0.15–0.90 ea). Puts a message
   in front of the exact client. You actually do the migration (AI-assisted) → get paid
   to do customer research and learn edge cases. Highest signal. This IS the "land as a
   service first" play.
2. **Find the same archetype OFF Upwork** — GHL agency owners are very public:
   GHL Facebook groups, Skool communities, r/gohighlevel, LinkedIn, YouTube. The job
   posts told us WHO (marketing agencies onboarding clients into GHL); reach them where
   they're identifiable.
3. **Discovery calls** (Mom-Test style) — DM agency owners, no pitch, just learn.

`ghl_leads.csv` has the 46 live GHL job URLs we already paid to find — use for path 1
(apply) and to study real buyer language.

---

## Path 1 — Upwork proposal (to a live GHL migration job)

Keep it short, lead with the workflow-rebuild pain (not data-move), prove you get it.

> Hi — I help agencies move clients into GoHighLevel without the 2–4 week slog. The
> contact CSV is the easy part; the real time-sink is rebuilding [source CRM]'s
> automations into GHL's dynamic workflows by hand. I've built an AI-assisted process
> that re-architects those workflows and auto-QAs each one with a test contact before
> cutover, so nothing breaks at go-live.
>
> Quick q so I scope it right: how many active workflows/automations are in the source
> account, and which CRM are you coming from? Happy to do a paid pilot on one sub-account
> first so you can see the quality before committing the rest.

(Adjust per job. The "paid pilot on one sub-account" hook fits the agency repeat-buyer.)

## Path 2/3 — Discovery DM (off Upwork, GHL communities / LinkedIn)

NO pitch. You're learning. (Mom Test: ask about their life, not your idea.)

> Hey [name] — I saw you run client onboarding into GoHighLevel. I'm researching how
> agencies handle CRM migrations and not trying to sell you anything. Could I ask you
> 4 quick questions about your last client migration? Even a voice note back would be
> hugely helpful.

### The 5 discovery questions (learn, don't lead)

1. Walk me through the **last** client you migrated into GHL — what did you actually do,
   start to finish?
2. Which step ate the most **time/hours**? (Listen for: workflow rebuild.)
3. What's broken or gone wrong at go-live? How do you QA it now?
4. Who does this work — you, a senior person, a freelancer? What does it cost you per
   client?
5. If a tool could do ONE step of that for you, which step would you hand over — and
   which would you *never* trust to software? (The "never trust" answer = the
   trust/liability risk; the "hand over" answer = the wedge.)

### What you're listening for (turns hypothesis → validated)

- ✅ "Rebuilding the workflows takes days" → confirms the value is where we think.
- ✅ "We do this every time we onboard a client" → confirms repeat-buyer.
- ✅ "I'd pay to not do X" → willingness to pay for a *product*, not just hire.
- 🚩 "I'd never let AI touch a client's live automations" → trust blocker; design for
  human-in-loop, sell QA/speed not full autonomy.
- 🚩 "Honestly the CSV import is most of it" → kills the workflow-rebuild thesis; rethink.

## Sequencing

Do **3–5 of Path 2/3 (free, this week)** BEFORE applying/building. Conversations answer
the product-vs-service question the scraper can't. If they confirm → run Path 1 (paid
pilot on one sub-account) as the first real revenue + deepest learning.
