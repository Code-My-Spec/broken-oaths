## Overview / bio

**Most GHL migrations break in the same place: the automations.**

Moving contacts is the easy part — a CSV does that. The real work is rebuilding
your old CRM's workflows inside GoHighLevel, because GHL's logic is completely
different from Keap, HubSpot, ActiveCampaign or Dubsado. "Lift and shift" leaves you
with dead automations, broken pipelines, and leads falling through the cracks on
go-live day.

I specialize in exactly that step. I migrate agencies and their client sub-accounts
into GoHighLevel and **re-architect every automation into GHL's workflow model**,
then QA each one with a live test contact before cutover — so nothing breaks when you
flip the switch.

**What I do:**
- Full CRM → GoHighLevel migration (contacts, custom fields, tags, pipelines, opportunities)
- Rebuild automations/campaigns as GHL workflows (email, SMS, wait steps, conditional logic)
- Agency sub-account setup & snapshot deployment for repeat client onboarding
- Field mapping from relational CRMs (HubSpot, Salesforce, Keap) into GHL's contact-centric model
- Pre-cutover QA: every workflow tested with a live contact

**How I'm faster:** I use an AI-assisted process to map fields and translate workflow
logic between platforms, which cuts a 2–4 week migration down to days — without
cutting corners on testing. You get agency-speed onboarding without your senior
person hand-rebuilding workflows for every new client.
