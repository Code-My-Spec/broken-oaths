## The market (read the room)

Three tiers exist at once, and they reveal the gap:
- **DIY tools $20–99/mo** (Otterly, Airefs, Rankscale, Scrunch, Averi): MONITOR, mostly
  don't DO. Founder learns "you're invisible in ChatGPT" → still can't fix it.
- **Fiverr/Upwork gigs $150–5k**: fragmented, low-trust, race-to-bottom.
- **GEO agencies $3,000–25,000+/mo** (Omnius, Skale): real money, but priced for funded
  cos; entry tier explicitly leaves implementation TO THE CLIENT.

**The gap = the DOING, at the bottom of the market.** Tools tell you you're invisible;
agencies are too expensive and make you implement anyway; the free plugin (claude-seo)
does the work but your ICP can't install a Claude Code plugin + MCP ("too much friction,"
"way beyond me" — their words in the thread).

## Honest cautions

- "Service forever" trap (same as GHL): set exit condition — after ~5 paying clients,
  productize the recurring parts of the workflow.
- One-off referral ≠ repeatable system yet. First clients prove the repeatable process.
